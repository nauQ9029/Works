step 4
Kiểm tra với posman
url: http://localhost:3000/api/items
create new item:
curl -X POST -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"name":"Item 3","description":"Description of Item 3"}' http://localhost:3000/api/items
update item
curl -X PUT -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"name":"Updated Item 1","description":"Updated Description"}' http://localhost:3000/api/items/1
delete item
curl -X DELETE http://localhost:3000/api/items/1
